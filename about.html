Our Story

VIQ Auto's was founded in 2026 with a simple mission: to make car buying transparent, affordable, and enjoyable. What started as a passion for quality vehicles has grown into a trusted dealership serving customers across Nigeria. We belive that everyone deserves a reliable car without the stress of traditional car shopping.

Our Mission

To provide quality, affordable cars with exceptional customer service, honest guidance, and a transparent, stress-free buying experience that builds lasting trust with every driver. We are committed to offering a carefully curated selection of top-tier cars from trusted brands, ensuring that every vehicle meets rigorous standards of safety, performance, and value. Our goal is to empower our customers with the knowledge and confidence they need to make informed decisions, and to support them long after they drive off our lot because at VIQ Auto's, your satisfaction is our driving force.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and customer-focused car dealership in Nigeria a brand synonymous with integrity, reliability, and excellence. We aspire to transform the car-buying experience by putting our customers at the heart of everything we do, offering transparent pricing, quality vehicles, and personalized service that exceeds expectations. Through innovation, continuous improvement, and a deep commitment to our community, we aim to set the standard for automotive retail in Nigeria earning the trust of every customer, one car at a time.

Our Values

  • Quality Guaranteed - Every car is thoroughly inspected
  • Transparency - No hidden fees, no surprises
  • Customer First - Your satisfaction is our number one priority
  • Intergrity - We do what we say we will do
